## ✨ Typography & Font Weight Optimization Status

Sabhi 22 pages ke text aur headings ke font weights ko modern, clean Poppins typography guidelines ke anusaar optimize aur reduce kar diya gaya hai:
- **Zero Heavy Font Weights**: Project ke sabhi pages me se bulky `font-black` (900) aur `font-extrabold` (800) ko hata diya gaya hai.
- **Modern Heading Standards**: Page titles aur section headers ab clean `font-bold` (700) aur `font-semibold` (600) me render ho rahe hain.
- **Body & Subtext Readability**: Paragraphs, subtitles, pills, badges aur button text ab lightweight `font-medium` (500) / `font-normal` (400) me crystal-clear read hoti hain bina kisi visual bleeding ke.
